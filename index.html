Controls

World map

  • Click a square to travel there, or step with WASD / arrows / numpad (Q E Z C diagonals).
  • Walk onto a town, cave or ruins to visit it.

Combat

  • The marching ants show where the active character can still move. Click inside to walk there.
  • Click an enemy (sword cursor) to walk up and attack.
  • Click a fallen ally (open hand) to try to stabilise them.
  • Space ends the turn early. Backspace or U rewinds the last action.

Poor Fantasy

A personal homage to the late-1980s Gold Box games, on the current D&D rules. Version 0.2.
